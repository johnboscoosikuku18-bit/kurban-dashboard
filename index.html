1. Click Settings tab → left sidebar: Pages

3. Wait 60 seconds → your dashboard is live at:
https://johnboscoosikuku18-bit.github.io/kurban-dashboard

Connect live KoboToolbox data
1. Open your live dashboard URL

3. Paste your new API token (after regenerating it)
